The 3-Card Spread: Past, Present & Future

The three-card tarot spread is one of the most powerful and classic layouts used by professional cartomancers. It offers a quick yet deep overview of your life path:

  • The Past (1st Card): Reveals the underlying energies, past decisions, and hidden events that shaped your current situation.
  • The Present (2nd Card): Highlights your current state of mind, immediate challenges, and opportunities happening right now.
  • The Future (3rd Card): Shows the most likely outcome based on your current path and how the energies will unfold if you continue in this direction.

How to Get the Most Accurate Tarot Reading

To get the most out of your AI tarot session, we recommend following these simple steps before drawing your cards:

  1. Clear Your Mind: Take three deep breaths and let go of daily distractions.
  2. Focus on Your Question: Ask an open-ended question rather than a simple "yes or no". For example, instead of "Will I get the job?", ask "What do I need to focus on to succeed in my career?".
  3. Trust Your Intuition: When you click on the cards, let your intuition guide your hand. The card that calls to you is the one meant for you.
